One sentence explaining the modern internet to a traveler from the year 1800, written for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb) bounty ($2 prize pool).

## The entry

> A town crier who never sleeps stands in each square on earth at once, shouting the news of all the world into your kitchen and answering whatever you shout back.

The image is a town crier — a figure anyone from 1800 already knows. He never sleeps, stands in each square on earth at once, and shouts the news of all the world into your kitchen, while answering whatever you shout back. News in, answers back: the whole of the internet in one picture.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the sentence to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) with no trailing newline.
